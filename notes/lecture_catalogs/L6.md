## Lecture 6 — Classification

Lecture 6 (57 slides, Dacheng Xiu, 18 Sep 2026) covers binary and multi-class classification. It opens with **logistic regression**: the logit link, statsmodels `glm` with a Binomial family, odds multipliers, MLE and deviance, deviance R² in sample and on a random left-out sample, gradient descent, and prediction. It then sets up the classification framework, the **Bayes classifier** and the parametric/non-parametric split, and teaches **K-nearest neighbours** (hand-coded and in sklearn; scaling; sensitivity to K; pros and cons). A **Lending Club** case follows, with a **date-based train/test split** and preprocessing learned on training data only, where KNN and logit are compared. The lecture then turns probabilities into decisions: the 0.5 Bayes rule, asymmetric costs and a cost-matrix threshold, the confusion matrix, FPR/FNR, sensitivity and specificity, ROC and AUC (in-sample vs test), precision–recall with rare events, precision@k and lift. It ends with a **checklist for reporting a classifier** (p.57). No slide covers trees, forests, boosting, CV code, class weights, calibration, LDA/QDA or multinomial logit.

**Bottom line for Problem 3.** P3 is a regression problem on a continuous vol-scaled target, and **no Lecture 6 estimator or classification metric is used anywhere in it** (the leakage audit says "no classifier in Problem 3", cell 91). P3 does apply several L6 *principles*: the date-ordered split, preprocessing learned on training data only, test-sample metrics next to in-sample ones, a small benchmark model, and a reporting checklist. It grounds all of them in L5 or the AI guide and **never cites L6** (every L6 citation in the notebook is in Problem 1). The rows below give page-level detail.    - Spot check (ours): an M1-like forecast is positive in 97.7% of OOS rows and hits 52.3% against 51.9% for "always up". This is the p.36/p.51 lesson in P3's own data, and it confirms Q1 without changing it.
   - Cost: trivial. It also closes the loop with P1 1.5.
3. **A logit robustness model** (L6 p.3–13, p.38).
   - Model: `smf.glm(..., family=Binomial())` (the house style, unpenalised) or `LogisticRegression(C=np.inf)`, never the plain default (AI guide §4c; L6 p.38).
   - Features and target: M2 features on the above-median label, fitted per expanding window.
   - Scoring: test-sample AUC and accuracy vs the majority rate. Optionally, (P̂ − 0.5)/σ̂ as P3-style weights.
   - Value: modest. Ridge already sits at the no-signal end in 64% of refits (Table 3.14), and the model adds a post-hoc 39th specification.
   - Leakage risk: any threshold or C tuned outside the forward inner folds.
