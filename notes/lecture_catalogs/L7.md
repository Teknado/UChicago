3. **NIT (related; our construction, not an L7 method). The effective N assumes the classes are independent.** Cell 52 computes the class-level effective N = Σ n_c / (1 + (n_c − 1)ρ_c) = 8.08, which treats the classes as uncorrelated. Table 3.2 shows correlations of 0.22–0.28 among A, B and C in 2003–2010. The true effective N is therefore lower, and the reported t of 1.01 for a class-level signal is optimistic. That strengthens the notebook's conclusion rather than weakening it. One clause would disclose it.
